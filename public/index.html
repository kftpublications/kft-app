About this concept

Concept prototype for discussion, 16 November 2026.

Content is live from KFT's podcast feeds (Podbean), YouTube channels, kfoundation.org and krishnamurticentre.org.uk.

In a production build: podcasts via RSS, videos via the YouTube Data API, articles and transcripts via the kfoundation.org WordPress API.

In the native app, videos play through YouTube's official embedded player, and booking, articles, store and donations open in an in-app browser, so nothing leaves the app.

Could ship as new tabs in Krishnamurti Daily, or as a new app that absorbs Daily's passage as its Today screen.
